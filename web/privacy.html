Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spurth, we collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account details — username, email address, password
  • Profile information — full name, birth date, gender, bio, location, profile photo
  • Activity content — activities you create or join, posts, comments, poll votes
  • Communications — messages sent through the app's chat features

We also collect certain information automatically:

  • Device location (with your permission), used to show nearby activities
  • Basic usage data, to help us maintain and improve the Service

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Show you relevant activities near you
  • Verify your account via email or phone
  • Communicate with you about your account or activities
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. How We Share Information

We don't sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With other users, as part of normal app functionality (e.g. your profile and posts are visible to other users)
  • With service providers who help us operate Spurth (e.g. cloud hosting, email delivery)
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your profile information directly within the app. For account deletion or other data requests, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Spurth is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
